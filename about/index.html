About

A little more about me.

My background is mostly systems and networking, with more recent professional and personal experience in security. I currently work as a Security Analyst in an MSP environment, where security work is mixed in with systems, networking, support, and general troubleshooting.

Where I am now

Security is where I want to keep growing.

Most of my recent personal time has been focused on penetration testing. I recently completed HTB's CPTS, which showed me that I still need to improve my methodology, note taking, and reporting. I am also starting to prepare for OSCP and have started focusing more on Active Directory attacks to get ready for it.

I am also spending time exploring DFIR, threat intelligence, OSINT, and threat hunting. I have not settled on one narrow specialty, but I want to keep moving toward more hands-on security work and keep building on the systems and networking background I already have.
